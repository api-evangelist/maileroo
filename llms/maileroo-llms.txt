# Maileroo

Maileroo is an all-in-one email delivery platform that provides SMTP relay, REST API, email marketing, email verification, and inbound email routing.

## Overview

Maileroo enables developers and businesses to send transactional and marketing emails with high deliverability, real-time tracking, and scalable infrastructure. ([Maileroo][1])

Core capabilities:

* SMTP relay for transactional email delivery
* REST Email API for programmatic sending
* Email marketing platform (campaigns, segmentation, templates)
* Email verification to reduce bounce rates
* Inbound email routing
* Real-time analytics (opens, clicks, bounces, events)
